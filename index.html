MediFlow — System Architecture & Workflow

MediFlow was conceptualized and developed during the NEXUS 2026 hackathon to eliminate chaotic bottlenecks in hospital outpatient departments (OPDs) by replacing physical queues with intelligent digital triage tokens.

Core Workflow Pipeline:

  1. Intake & Registration: Receptionist or kiosk logs patient symptoms and category (e.g., General, Senior, Emergency).
  2. Token Generation: A unique prioritized token (e.g., #OPD-104) is generated with timestamped sequencing.
  3. Dynamic Load Balancer: The routing engine checks doctor consultation room availability and patient load.
  4. Live Waiting Room Monitor: Real-time display updates sound an alert when the patient's token is called to Counter 3.
  5. Consultation Handshake: Staff mark the session active, automatically advancing the next patient in sequence.
